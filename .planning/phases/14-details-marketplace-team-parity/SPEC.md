# Phase 14 — Details, Connect Apps & Team Bot Parity

## Objective

Make Bot management and app/team setup follow the same visible hierarchy and step flow as the pinned Grok Bot reference.

This phase inherits Phase 10 UI-SPEC.md.

## Scope

- Conversation Details home and subnavigation
- Tasks, Routines, Library, Settings, sharing entry placement
- Connect apps / plugin discovery dialog
- featured plugin/logo treatment and reference wording
- Team Bot setup flow, progress indicator, publish state
- Team Bot managers/roles where present in the reference
- per-Bot draft policy control where present
- secure credential/login request UX where present
- loading/error/connected/disconnected states

## Acceptance Criteria

A Grok Bot user can manage a Bot, connect apps, and configure/publish a Team Bot without encountering a different information architecture.

## DONE Gate

All details, plugin, sharing, and Team Bot canonical flows pass parity and authorization verification.
